# Za govor! — Visual Art Direction

## Core principle

The game's visual identity depends on a deliberate contrast between **restrained 16-bit gameplay pixel art** and **refined Slovenian folk-art storytelling cards**. This contrast must remain consistent across characters, enemies, environments, skills and future content.

## Gameplay: 8-bit to 16-bit, not beyond

The goal is a modest, controlled upgrade of the existing 8-bit art toward SNES/GBA-inspired 16-bit RPG pixel art. Preserve the established scale, proportions, simple readable silhouettes and visual personality. Improve selective shading, volume, palette richness, pose clarity and animation expression. Do not jump to 32-bit/HD pixel art, painterly rendering, realistic illustration or high-detail modern pixel art. The Martin Krpan 8-bit-to-16-bit comparison is the primary calibration reference.

"16-bit" describes the visual language, not a literal palette or technical colour-depth constraint. Pixel density and detail must be consistent and readable at actual gameplay size, especially on mobile.

## Folk art: a separate storytelling medium

Skill cards, collectible illustrations and selected narrative artwork use a refined illustrated style inspired by Slovenian *panjske končnice* and traditional folk painting: confident contours, expressive simplified figures, flat pigment-like colour, warm parchment/wood tones, restrained ornament and clear story composition. They are not pixel art. Generated illustrations are original game artwork inspired by this tradition, not authentic historical artefacts.

The distinction is intentional: gameplay is interactive, compact and readable; folk-art panels are crafted, narrative and decorative.

## Environments

Regional environments use the same restrained 16-bit pixel language as gameplay. Build scenes from independently composable layers when parallax or animation is needed (for example sky, distant terrain, town/castle, river, near bank and ground). Water, reflections and occasional foliage details may animate separately. Keep geographic relationships credible and verify real locations from reliable sources.

## UX and composition

Preserve clean gameplay space and strong readability. The interactive gameplay area remains dominant (approximately three quarters of the composition where the established layout applies), with story/context occupying the smaller remaining area. Use dark surfaces and restrained gold accents/frames where UI treatment is needed. Avoid decorative clutter and misleading visual affordances.

## Production and approval

Use existing assets and implementation as the baseline. GPT develops visual concepts, research and asset specifications; Claude implements approved work and supplies technical previews/tests; the Game Director approves creative direction. Never invent folklore facts, mechanics, weapons, landmarks or character details. Mark content CONFIRMED, IMPLEMENTED, PROPOSED, OPEN or SUPERSEDED.
