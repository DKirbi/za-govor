# Additional folk skill-card concepts

**Status:** Basic generated concepts for characters outside the first reviewed group. Pending Game Director review; not approved or production-ready.

These six single-scene images use the compact painted Za govor! battle-card style referenced in [`battle-desktop.png`](../images/battle-desktop.png). Each image remains a concept and is separate from runtime exports.

## Lepa Vida

- **Hrepenenje:** Vida sings by the shore; one foe hesitates. Laundry and clothing cues echo her ballad identity.
- **Morska pena:** A restrained sea-foam curl accompanies her song and delays one foe. Vida is not portrayed as an elemental water caster.
- **Pesem z obale:** Her song reaches several foes from the shore. The scene does not depict a particular ballad ending.

Preserve the differences among collected ballad variants. Do not present the Piran game setting as the ballad's origin or import unrelated Vida-and-snake material.

## Martin Krpan

- **Lipov kij:** Krpan swings his limewood cudgel at one foe.
- **Kmečka moč:** The image depicts the rank-2 *Vreči soli* presentation: Krpan throws two salt sacks while his small mare stands safely beside him. The mare is not an attacker. This rank image is gameplay fiction, distinct from the literary source.
- **Mesarica:** Krpan makes a precise sword strike. Any red mark is a small gameplay cue for bleed, not gore.

Keep Krpan grounded in Levstik's literary character, not a generic fantasy barbarian. The existing separate folk-art subject of Krpan lifting his mare and the confirmed Brdavs ending card remain separate narrative pieces.
