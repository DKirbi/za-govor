## Final illustration: The sleeping king beneath Peca

Depict Kralj Matjaž asleep in the cave beneath Mount Peca, surrounded by his sleeping soldiers. His famous long beard is wrapped around the stone table.

The king, the beard and the sleeping company are the central subjects. The scene should feel quiet, timeless and expectant: the company is waiting for the moment when Matjaž will awaken and return to help his people.

## Folklore grounding

The sleeping king beneath Peca, his sleeping warriors, the stone table and his beard winding around it are documented motifs in the Kralj Matjaž tradition. The number of turns is variable between versions; do not make a precise number visually prominent unless the selected source version is specified.

Do not add the winter linden to this cave scene by default. It belongs to some tellings of the awakening motif and may be better suited to the *Prebujenje* skill illustration.
