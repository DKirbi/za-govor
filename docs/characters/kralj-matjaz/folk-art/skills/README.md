# Kralj Matjaž — skill folk-art

**Status:** Three subjects CONFIRMED; artwork deferred until the full roster review is complete.

Create three separate traditional folk-art illustrations, one for each skill. The panjske končnice tradition is an important visual reference for the project's folk-art treatment. These should be distinct narrative images, not three near-identical portraits with different labels.

## 1. Meč

Depict Kralj Matjaž delivering a direct sword strike. The composition should communicate a decisive, grounded attack and the king's role as a protector of his company. Rank 2 and rank 3 add party protection mechanically, but do not add arbitrary magical armour or invented symbols unless approved.

## 2. Kraljeva vojska

Depict Matjaž rallying and inspiring his soldiers. The company should read as his loyal warriors responding to their king's call. Emphasise collective morale and readiness rather than depicting a generic army charge. The damage bonus and enemy weakening are gameplay effects, not claims from the legend.

## 3. Prebujenje

Depict the king awakening and restoring his company. His soldiers begin to rise with him; the composition can suggest renewed strength and protection. The rank-3 attack against all enemies is a gameplay effect and should not force an invented folkloric event into the image. A winter linden coming into leaf may be included as a documented motif from some versions, but is not mandatory.

## Art direction constraints

- Traditional folk-art illustration, with panjske končnice as a reference; not gameplay pixel art.
- Keep Matjaž recognisable and visually consistent across all three images.
- His crown, long beard, red cloak and sword are existing prototype design cues, not independently verified historical costume.
- Keep the visual storytelling rooted in the Peca tradition.
- Do not generate artwork until the full roster review is complete.
