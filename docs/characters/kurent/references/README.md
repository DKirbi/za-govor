# Kurent — folklore and visual references

## Research status

Initial research direction recorded; detailed source verification is still required before final art production or any precise historical claims.

Prioritise:
- UNESCO's inscription and description of the Door-to-door rounds of Kurenti.
- Slovenski etnografski muzej collections and documentation of kurentija, masks, bells and ježevka.
- Pokrajinski muzej Ptuj-Ormož and Kurentovanje / local ethnographic documentation.
- Scholarly ethnological research on regional variants, historical changes and the tradition's origins.

## Research cautions

- Kurent is a living carnival and folklore figure. Avoid presenting speculative pre-Christian origins as established fact.
- Costume and headwear vary between local types and over time.
- Ježevka and bells are traditional objects; the combat abilities in the game are original gameplay interpretation.
- Verify the devil figure in the final story-card composition against reliable Kurentovanje documentation before depicting it as an authentic procession element.
