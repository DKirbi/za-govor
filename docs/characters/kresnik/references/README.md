# Kresnik — research references

## Scholarly folklore research

1. **Monika Kropej**, “From Tradition to Contemporary Belief Tales: The ‘Changing Life’ of Some Slovenian Supernatural Beings from the Annual Cycle,” *Studia mythologica Slavica* 13 (2010), pp. 171–185.  
   https://ojs.zrc-sazu.si/sms/article/download/1646/1391/4336  
   Especially pp. 171–173: Kresnik and midsummer; fire and sun; the royal hero, dragon/snake combat, rescue and harvest motifs; regional variants; transformation of the figure over time.

2. **Zmago Šmitek**, “Kresnik: An Attempt at a Mythological Reconstruction,” *Studia mythologica Slavica* 1 (1998), pp. 93–118.  
   https://ojs.zrc-sazu.si/sms/article/download/1867/1614/  
   Comparative interpretation of the Kresnik cycle and its possible relationship to thunder-god myths. The author explicitly notes uncertainty and disagreement in the scholarship; use as an interpretation, not settled fact.

3. **Zmago Šmitek**, “Od staroslovanskega Svarožiča/Radogosta do slovenskega Kresnika,” *Studia mythologica Slavica* 13 (2010), pp. 187–197.  
   https://doi.org/10.3986/sms.v13i0.1647  
   Discusses the possible conceptual relationship between Svarožič/Radogost, fire, sun, war, and Slovenian Kresnik traditions. Do not collapse these figures into one without qualification.

4. **Helena Ložar-Podlogar**, “Kres. Die Sonnwendbräuche der Slowenen / Slovenske kresne šege,” *Studia mythologica Slavica* 1 (1998), pp. 225–242.  
   https://doi.org/10.3986/sms.v1i0.1876  
   Research on Slovenian midsummer customs, bonfires, processions, songs, plants and Christianised practice.

## Research cautions

- Kresnik is a layered and regionally variable figure, not a single fully consistent mythological biography.
- Fire, sparks, sun and midsummer are well-supported associations.
- Dragon / snake combat and rescue narratives are documented, but individual episodes belong to particular variants.
- The identification of Kresnik with Perun or another reconstructed deity is debated scholarship.
- The fern flower and burning wheel in the existing prototype art note have not yet been verified as Kresnik-specific motifs.
