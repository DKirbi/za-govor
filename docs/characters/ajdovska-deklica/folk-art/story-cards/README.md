# Ajdovska deklica — folklore story cards

These cards tell the documented folklore behind the character. Keep them distinct from skill depictions.

## The Prophecy
A folk-art depiction of Ajdovska deklica as a sojenica, with the prophecy connected to Zlatorog.

## The Mountain Protector
A folk-art depiction of her guiding and helping travellers lost in mountain snowstorms.

## Special unlock — The Stone Wall
A unique folk-art depiction of her petrified form / the stone face at Prisank.

Unlock condition: Ajdovska deklica must remain in the active party until the player defeats the final boss.

The special card is a game reward and should not imply that the source folklore describes this exact game ending.
